MEIBO's Remarkable Presence at the Hong Kong Consumer Electronics Show 2025

Oct 31, 2025

MEIBO was proud to participate in the Hong Kong Consumer Electronics Show 2025, presenting our latest lineup of premium tempered glass screen protectors to distributors, retailers and brand partners from around the world.

Showcasing Innovation

Visitors to our booth experienced firsthand the clarity, hardness and precision fit of our flagship series — including Fingerprint Unlock Glass, UV Glass and the MEIBO BASIC Series. Live demonstrations highlighted our bubble-free installation and anti-fingerprint performance.

Strengthening Global Partnerships

The event provided a valuable platform to connect with new and existing partners across more than 100 countries. Our team shared updates on OEM/ODM capabilities, rapid fulfilment from our 3,000㎡ inventory, and the marketing support we provide to channel partners.

Looking Ahead

Building on the momentum of the show, MEIBO remains committed to "innovation for development, quality for survival, and integrity for customers" — continuing to deliver reliable, high-quality protection to smartphone users worldwide.
